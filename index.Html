Miniatures Club!!!

The main goal of the Miniatures Club is to provide students with a creative and social space to explore tabletop miniature hobbies. Members can build, paint, customize, and play games using miniatures while developing creativity, patience, problem-solving, strategic thinking, and collaboration with other students.
